# Scenario: AI-Assisted SBA Express-Style Loan Evidence Trace

A small business begins a digital loan application. The workflow touches
multiple systems:

1. Digital session and consent capture
2. KYB and fraud screening
3. AI-assisted application intake
4. Document upload and extraction
5. Credit analysis and policy matching
6. Human underwriting review
7. Embedded banking partner routing
8. Loan decision
9. Audit replay bundle generation

The goal is to show how BIL / INK creates a portable evidence trail across
systems that would otherwise keep separate logs, payload hashes, policy
references, and reviewer actions.

This scenario is synthetic. It is shaped for discussion with small-business
lending stakeholders and design partners, not for production certification.
