# Scenario: AI-Assisted SBA Express-Style Loan Evidence Trace

A small business begins a digital loan application. The workflow touches multiple
systems:

1. Digital session and consent capture
2. KYB and fraud screening
3. AI-assisted application intake
4. Document upload and extraction
5. Credit analysis and policy matching
6. Human review
7. Loan decision
8. Audit replay bundle generation

The goal is to show how BIL / INK creates a portable evidence trail across those systems.
